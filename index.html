About Elah Farm

From Kenyan Soil to Your Table

Elah Farm is based in Ikutha, Kitui County, Kenya. We celebrate agriculture, locally grown products and the value of connecting people with the land that sustains them.

Our farm experience gives visitors an opportunity to explore rural life, learn about agricultural activities and enjoy a welcoming stay in the Kenyan countryside.

  • Locally rooted agricultural experiences
  • Farm learning and visitor activities
  • Kenyan products and community connections
