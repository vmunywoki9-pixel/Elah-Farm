About Elah Farm

Authentic products with a Kenyan farm story.

Elah Farm is based in Ikutha Kitui, Kenya, and is focused on bringing customers quality farm products through direct sales. Our range brings together the natural goodness of honey, cashew nuts, coffee and Galla goat meat.

We believe customers should know where their products come from and have an easy way to connect with the farm. That is why you can order directly from Elah Farm.
