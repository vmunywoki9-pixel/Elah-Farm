About Elah Farm

Growing Quality Products From Kenya

Elah Farm is a Kenyan agricultural enterprise based in Ikutha, Kitui County. We are committed to producing and sourcing quality farm products for customers in Kenya and beyond.

From natural honey and farm-fresh eggs to cashew nuts, coffee, goat meat and other agricultural products, our goal is to connect customers with quality products from Kenya.
