About Elah Farm

Rooted in Kenya. Growing with Purpose.

Our farm is built around quality, responsible farming and creating products that connect customers with the richness of Kenyan agriculture.

Quality products from the heart of Kitui.

Elah Farm is based in Ikutha, Kitui, Kenya. We are focused on producing and supplying carefully selected agricultural products while supporting local farming and sustainable livelihoods.

Our product range includes natural honey, cashew nuts, Kenyan coffee, Galla goat meat, bee venom, macadamia nuts, avocado oil and ground nuts.
